Two wandering rebels building a life together across galaxies.

Our journey began on the northern outpost of Växjö back in 2021, where we met while studying political science and ended up negotiating the ultimate treaty: falling in love. Later, we established our cozy rebel base in Malmö, before punching the hyperdrive and making the leap across the galaxy to the Netherlands with our cats. Even though Damlanur prefers every mission planned with strict Imperial precision, our 6 years together have been full of rogue asteroid fields and unexpected detours.

Now, we’re ready to lock in our alliance and gather our favorite rebels, allies, and Jedi for the next chapter. We aren't interested in stuffy conservative traditions—we just want to say “I love you” (and “I know”), feast on Dutch fries, and celebrate until the Death Star goes down.

Traveling from another galaxy

Transit intel & Victory tips for our guests traveling from another galaxy

Recommended Airports

Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) offers the direct flights from Sweden & Turkey, with frequent intercity trains to Venray (~1.5–2 hrs).

Düsseldorf (DUS) in Germany is also very close that offers direct flights from Sweden & Turkey, with frequent trains to Venray (~1 hr drive / train).

Trains & Transport

The Dutch rail network (NS.nl) is very easy to use. You can tap in and out with your contactless debit/credit card. Trains run regularly to Venray Station. Taxis and local buses connect directly to the town center.

Where to Stay

We recommend booking in or near the outpost Venray (e.g. Hotel Asteria, Hulsman or cozy local B&Bs ).

If you’d like recommendations or want to coordinate a convoy/carpool from the airport with fellow allies, just reach us!
