Traveling from another galaxy

Transit intel & master tips for our traveling allies

Recommended Airports

Düsseldorf (DUS) in Germany is very close with direct flights from Sweden & Turkey, with frequent trains to Venray (~1 hr drive / train).

Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) is also very close with direct flights and frequent trains to Venray (~1.5–2 hrs).

Trains & Transport

The Dutch rail network (NS.nl) is seamless, simply tap in and out with your contactless debit or credit card. Regular trains run directly to Venray Station.

Where to Stay

We recommend staying in or near Venray (e.g. Hotel Asteria, Hotel Hulsman, or local B&Bs).

Let us know if you want to coordinate carpools or rides from the airport with fellow allies!
