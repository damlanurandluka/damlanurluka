a long time ago in a galaxy far, far away...

It started six years ago when our paths crossed in Sweden. Since then, we decided to join forces, graduate school, and narrowly dodge a one-way ticket to Azkaban by making the jump to build our new home in the Netherlands alongside our beloved cats, Obi and Stitch.

So, if you decide not to turn us in to the Ministry of Magic for the reward, you are warmly invited to join the celebration for our next chapter. We are happily skipping traditions building our way with an intimate ceremony under the trees. We just want to sign the papers, tie the knot, eat some fries, and gather our favorite people under the stars.

Traveling from another galaxy

Recommended Airports

Eindhoven (EIN): Closest to Venray (~45–50 min drive/train) with frequent European flights.

Düsseldorf (DUS): Direct flights from Sweden & Turkey (~1 hr drive/train).

Amsterdam (AMS): Major hub with direct trains (~1.5–2 hrs).

Trains & Transit

From AMS: Direct train underneath terminal toward Nijmegen, switch to Arriva to Venray Station.

From EIN: Bus 400/401 to Eindhoven Centraal, then train toward Venray.

From DUS: Train/bus to Venlo, then connect to Venray. Tap in/out with contactless cards on NS.nl & 9292.nl.

Where to Stay

Things you should know

Questions & Answers

We would love to see you in colors and comfy clothes for the summer, like satin, linen, etc.! We also put together a little color palette we had in mind for inspiration. Feel free to wear whatever makes you feel good and ready to celebrate with us!

Footwear note: Since we'll be strolling along wooded park trails at Odapark, block heels, wedges, dress flats, or clean loafers are much friendlier on grass and gravel than sharp stilettos!

The ceremony and reception take place at Odapark! Afterwards, we will head over to the friethuis near our place for dinner, followed by the evening party at our home in Leunen. It is a quick trip by car, shared ride, walk, or bike, and free parking is available at all locations.
Dutch weather likes to keep everyone on their toes! If showers roll in, we will move smoothly under the covered veranda and warm rooms of Odapark Theehuis so everything stays cozy and dry.
We will have vegetarian and gluten-free choices available for dinner and snacks. Please select your preferences and mention any allergies in the RSVP section above so we can make sure there is plenty of good food for you.
Making the journey to celebrate with us is the greatest gift of all! If you’d still like to contribute something extra, a small contribution toward our honeymoon fund would mean the world to us.
