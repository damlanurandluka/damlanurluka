Traveling from another galaxy

Transit intel & Victory tips for our guests traveling from another galaxy

Recommended Airports

Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) offers direct flights from Sweden & Turkey, with frequent intercity trains to Venray (~1.5–2 hrs).

Düsseldorf (DUS) in Germany is also very close with direct flights from Sweden & Turkey, with frequent trains to Venray (~1 hr drive / train).

Trains & Transport

The Dutch rail network (NS.nl) is very easy to use. You can tap in and out with your contactless debit/credit card. Trains run regularly to Venray Station. Taxis and local buses connect directly to the town center.

Where to Stay

We recommend booking in or near the outpost Venray (e.g. Hotel Asteria, Hulsman, or cozy local B&Bs).

If you’d like recommendations or want to coordinate a convoy/carpool from the airport with fellow allies, just reach out to us!
